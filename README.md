# MP3 Audio Player using Arduino 
It is a MP3 Audio Player using Arduino ...It has a display to show the song, push buttons to pause/play ,to play next song or to play the previous song, a rotatory wheel to increase or decrease the volume and also it is RECHARGABLE (With a c cable)


## What it does:
Its got a 1.8" TFT LCD (SPI) to display the song you're listening to
It has  push buttons to pause/play ,to play next song or to play the previous song
A rotatory wheel to increase or decrease the volume

## Materials reqired:
### 🔹 Core
- Arduino Nano
- DFPlayer Mini
- Micro SD Card
  
### 🔹 Display
- 1.8" TFT LCD (SPI)
  
### 🔹 Audio
- 3.5mm headphone jack
- Wired earphones
  
### 🔹 Power (Rechargeable)
- 3.7V Li-ion / Li-Po battery
- TP4056 charging module (with protection)
- Slide power switch
- MT3608 boost converter (to 5V)
  
### 🔹 Controls
- Push buttons (Play / Next / Previous)
- rotary encoder (volume)
